About ViaMontana

A little planning.
A world of mountains.

ViaMontana helps you put together hut-to-hut hiking trips in the Swiss Alps. Explore the trails, find your next hut, and build a journey that fits the way you like to walk.

Plan your trip →

Your trip, one hut at a time

Start with a mountain hut, set your preferred hiking time and elevation change, then choose your next hike on the map. Or use Find itineraries to explore options for a multiday trip.

See the whole journey

Compare walking time, distance, ascent, descent, and hiking categories. Where available, access walks connect the first and last huts to public transport, helping you plan beyond the hut-to-hut stages.

Built around Swiss trails

Routes follow swisstopo’s hiking trail network, with a choice of swisstopo and OpenStreetMap base maps. Walking times are estimates; unverified connections are shown separately and excluded from trip totals.
